Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order.
How can I place an order?

Add your preferred items to the cart, enter your delivery details and press “Order on WhatsApp”.

What are the serving hours?

Zafaa Foods serves lunch from 12:30 PM to 4:00 PM. Advance ordering is recommended.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery is available in selected areas of Delhi. Delivery availability and charges are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Which payment methods are accepted?

You can select UPI or Cash while placing the order. Final confirmation is shared on WhatsApp.

Can I order multiple items together?

Yes. Add multiple dishes to the cart, adjust the quantities and send the complete order in one WhatsApp message.
